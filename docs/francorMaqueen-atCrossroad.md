# at crossroad [m]

What the robot does at a crossroad (left, right and straight).

## Example

```blocks
francorMaqueen.start()
francorMaqueen.atCrossroad(francorMaqueen.CrossroadRule.Straight)
francorMaqueen.patrol(francorMaqueen.OnOff.On)
```

## Parameters

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `m` | What to do |
