# at T-junction [m]

What the robot does at a T-junction (left and right).

## Example

```blocks
francorMaqueen.start()
francorMaqueen.atTJunction(francorMaqueen.TRule.Left)
francorMaqueen.patrol(francorMaqueen.OnOff.On)
```

## Parameters

| Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `m` | What to do |
